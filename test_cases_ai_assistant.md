

## 8. Sample Test Prompts (Questions to Ask)

Use these questions to verify the AI's legal knowledge and retrieval capabilities.

### General Legal Knowledge (Indian Context)
*   "What are the fundamental rights guaranteed by the Indian Constitution?"
*   "Explain the difference between Culpable Homicide and Murder under IPC."
*   "What are the rights of an arrested person in India?"
*   "Define 'Medical Negligence' under Consumer Protection Act."

### Specific Acts & Sections
*   "What does Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act deal with?"
*   "Explain Section 498A of the Indian Penal Code."
*   "What are the grounds for divorce under the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955?"
*   "What is the punishment for cyberstalking under the IT Act?"

### Procedural Questions
*   "What is the procedure to file an FIR online?"
*   "How can I file a consumer complaint against a defective product?"
*   "What is the limitation period for filing a suit for recovery of money?"
*   "Explain the process of anticipatory bail."

### Document Analysis (RAG Testing)
*   *(Upload a sample rental agreement PDF)* -> "Summarize the termination clauses in this agreement."
*   *(Upload a court judgment PDF)* -> "What was the final verdict in this case?"
*   *(Upload a contract)* -> "List all the obligations of the 'Service Provider' mentioned in this document."
